### Equip, deploy and stow
- Satisfactory game audio by **Coffee Stain Studios**.
- Deploy: `Play_UI_Inventory_Drop`; stow: `Play_UI_Inventory_Drag`.
- Referenced from the installed game; no stock audio media copied into the mod.
  Ordinary inventory equip/unequip feedback is handled by Satisfactory itself.
- Fallback rising/falling chirps are original procedural synthesis for this mod,
  generated by `scripts/hoverboard_audio_production.py`; no third-party samples.

### Landing
- Custom landing audio is disabled. Satisfactory's normal player landing audio
  remains game-managed. Hoverboard landing detection is retained for future use.

### Local research and unused auditions - not release assets
- Unselected recordings, original downloads, mixes and their metadata are retained
  under `Content/Audio/JetBoard/unused/`.
- Full third-party audition credits remain in
  [the audio attribution list](Content/Audio/JetBoard/attribution.md), and derived
  mix sources/edits remain in [the archived manifest](Content/Audio/JetBoard/unused/mixes/manifest.json).
- Jak II BOARD.SBK samples were extracted through OpenGOAL for private reference.
  OpenGOAL's code license does not license the original game's audio. These samples
  and derivatives must stay out of distributed packages.
- `unused/Jak_TPL_Orb_Final.ogg` is an unselected local reference with no supplied
  source/license record; it is not approved release audio.
- Since 0.1.26, the runtime profile/catalog use only `Audio/JetBoard/Production`.
  TestSounds, Diagnostics and JetBoard/unused are excluded with Unreal Never Cook.
  The fresh package inventory and extracted PCM passed the production audio audit.
